# Contributor licence agreement

**Status: not yet available. Do not sign anything from this file.**

EmbStudio requires every contributor, the EmbStudio developer included, to sign a contributor
licence agreement before their first contribution is merged (specification, section 10).

- The agreement is to be drafted with the Apache Software Foundation's Individual Contributor
  License Agreement as a model, and checked by a lawyer before anyone signs.
- Signatures will be recorded by a CLA bot on pull requests.
- Separately, the project owner and the EmbStudio developer agree in writing on credit and on
  any future revenue, before the developer's first merge.

This file will hold the agreement's text and how to sign it once it is ready.
